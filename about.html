About

Hi! I'm Nikos, a designer and researcher who came into design sideways, from engineering, and stayed for the human part of it.

I recently wrapped up a year as a UX Designer and Scrum Master at Ericsson in Gothenburg, working on dense private-5G software where the job was making genuinely complicated tools clear for expert users. Most of what I'm proudest of there sat between design and engineering: a tool I built that makes Figma files readable by AI coding agents, a set of co-creation methods I created and presented with a colleague at the Ericsson Developer Conference, and a generative UI proposal for data-dense dashboards. Before that I did a Master's in Interaction Design and Technologies at Chalmers, where my thesis turned into a museum installation that has been running in two Gothenburg museums since 2025.

I'm passionate about music, sound, light, and interfaces, and how they intersect with design to create immersive, emotional experiences. I like living in the in-between of design, technology, and human connection.
