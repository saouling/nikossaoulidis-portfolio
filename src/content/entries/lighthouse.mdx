Light rigs, the programmable lights that shape a live show, usually need complex software to control their effects. That complexity locks smaller venues and less experienced operators out of tools built for professionals.

I designed LightHouse to balance advanced functionality with an interface that stays calm: group lights, build and manage sequences, and adjust colour, intensity and movement in real time. It supports both a timeline mode for programming a show and a live mode for running one, with a 3D stage preview so you can see a change before the audience does.

Market research into existing lighting control showed where they fall short, and that shaped the priorities: legible in low light and loud rooms, a modular workspace, and quick actions for the pace of a live performance, without losing a newcomer on the first screen. I also designed its visual identity, including the logo.
