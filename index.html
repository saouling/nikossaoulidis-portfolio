I came into design sideways, from engineering, and stayed for the human part of it. I've shipped dense enterprise software at Ericsson, redesigned a platform now used across 2,700+ institutions, and built a museum installation that's been running for over a year.

I like living in the in-between of design, technology, and human connection.
