EWP Dashboard

Redesigning the tool European universities use to manage Erasmus+ mobility, as one coherent system instead of five disconnected modules.

Role
Lead UI/UX Designer
When
2022–2023 (redesign), earlier as intern 2019–2022
Where
Aristotle University IT Center, Thessaloniki, with the European University Foundation
Type
Redesign at scale, design system

Now the gold standard across 2,700+ institutions in 31 countries. My part was leading the redesign early on, not the scale it's reached since.

The problem

The EWP Dashboard is a tool higher education institutions across Europe use to manage Erasmus+ mobility students. The previous version had significant usability issues, a major barrier for hundreds of institutions relying on it. As my first large-scale UI/UX project, I took on redesigning the app to improve the experience, establish a cohesive visual identity, and lead the development process.

What I did

User research

To ground my design decisions, I designed and ran a user survey to gather feedback from the institutions using the tool. Data from hundreds of users shaped the foundation of the redesign. I later presented the results in a webinar, speaking to over 1,000 users and stakeholders from the European University Foundation.

Design system & visual identity

I built a design system, based on Bootstrap, as the foundation for the new interface, keeping it consistent and usable across every feature. I also designed the visual identity, including the logo, to refresh the app's branding.

Prototyping & development

I prototyped the redesigned dashboard in Figma and handed it to the development team, then oversaw development to make sure the final product matched the spec and stayed as user-friendly as intended.

Collaboration & leadership

I led a team of four developers, keeping constant communication between them and stakeholders so the technical build matched the design vision. It was new and valuable for me, challenging my communication and management skills in a diverse, agile team.

Outcome

The redesigned dashboard went back into another webinar and got strongly positive feedback. It's still used by hundreds of institutions across Europe to manage Erasmus+ mobilities and connect with the Erasmus+ Mobile App, with tasks now flowing more intuitively, with fewer errors and friction.

This project was a defining moment in my design career: research, design, leadership and communication, together, at a large-scale professional level.
