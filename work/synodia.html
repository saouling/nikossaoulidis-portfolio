Synodia

A slow, collective, touch-first music player, a quiet argument against passive, algorithm-driven streaming.

Role
Designer, individual project
When
2024
Where
Concept-Driven Interaction Design, Chalmers University of Technology
Type
Concept product, physical UI

An exploration of what listening together could feel like, not a shipped metric.

The problem

Streaming made music passive and algorithm-driven: endless, frictionless, and easy to skip past without really listening. Synodia is a portable music device designed to offer a slower, more intentional experience by letting you play just one song a day.

Synodia is slow

A portable music device that can only play one song for about a day, offering a slower, more intentional music experience.

Synodia is collective

Share songs with your synced friends, without algorithms dictating your choices, bringing back the personal connection in music sharing.

Synodia is touch

Feel the music through tangible interaction, a more intimate experience than the one lost in the era of smartphones and streaming.

Synodia is memorable

Liberates you from distraction, focusing on the only thing that matters, one song at a time, more memorable than an endless line of skipped songs.
