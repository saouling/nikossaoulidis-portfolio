# Surface brief: the Catalogue (whole site)

Scope: homepage, About, Contact, and the shared case-study template ([slug].astro + the three MDX studies). Visitor mode: Experience, read by recruiters skimming for ~90 seconds.

Audience and job: a hiring manager with several tabs open must place Nikos (role, strongest proof) within seconds, then optionally go deep on one object. Proof: DBAS still running in two museums; EWP live at 2,700+ institutions; Ericsson delivery method adopted team-wide.

Decisions locked with Nikos (2026-09-23): museum-catalogue structure; Kandinsky forms (circle = DBAS, square = EWP, triangle = Ericsson); display-case index images, with full-width plates above rows on phones; colour = job (blue interactive, red catalogue marks, gold only in forms and diagrams, museum red/blue coding scoped to DBAS); every critique fix; Ericsson as one page with sub-records 01a to 01c; DBAS prose condensed to at most ~1,500 words, with Nikos approving the new text.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Every project is an object in Nikos's collection: its catalogue label first, then its story. This refuses the category default of a poster hero over a card grid of screenshots.
OWN-WORLD: White ground, ink type, hairline rules. Jost for structure, with tabular catalogue numerals; Source Serif 4 for prose, and its italic for quotes. Three Kandinsky forms are the only large colour: a blue circle, a red square and a gold triangle. Red otherwise marks catalogue numbers, status and rules; blue marks links. Label plates are ink-bordered with sharp corners.
STORY: The visitor meets the index, reads three statuses, opens one object, reads its label and four-line summary, and leaves via the next object or contact.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Top left, a 64-72px name with one positioning line. Top right, the three forms as a still composition. Below, index rows 01 to 03 are fully visible (No. / title / year / medium / status). At right, the display case shows object 01; hovering or focusing a row swaps in its image and brings its form forward. CV and contact sit in the nav.
FORM: Museum catalogue, position 2 of 7 on the ordered list. Seed key b3ca529e.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
